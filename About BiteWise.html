What is BiteWise?

BiteWise is a modern nutrition and wellness app designed to help you understand what's on your plate and take control of your health — one meal at a time.

BiteWise combines AI-powered food recognition, personalized meal planning, and comprehensive nutrition tracking in one seamless experience. Whether you're trying to lose weight, build muscle, eat more mindfully, or manage a health condition, BiteWise adapts to your unique goals and preferences.

With support for Egyptian and international cuisines and real-time nutrition insights, BiteWise bridges the gap between everyday eating and long-term wellness.

Key highlights:

📸 AI Scanner — instantly identify food and get nutrition facts from a photo

📊 Smart Tracking — log meals, water, and progress with ease

🥗 Personalized Plans — meal suggestions tailored to your goals

🛒 Smart Shopping — auto-generated grocery lists from your meal plan
