Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How do I scan my food? ▾
Open the app and tap the Scan icon in the bottom navigation. Point your camera at the food item and hold steady. The AI will analyze the image and display nutrition information including calories, protein, carbs, and fat. You can also upload a photo from your gallery by tapping the gallery icon.
How does AI food analysis work? ▾
BiteWise uses a machine learning model trained on thousands of food images to identify ingredients and estimate portions. When you scan a meal, the AI analyzes visual features — color, texture, shape — and cross-references them with our nutrition database to provide accurate calorie and macronutrient estimates.
How do I save recipes? ▾
Browse recipes on the Discover page. Tap any recipe card to view details, then tap the Add to plan button or the heart icon (♥) to save it to your favorites. You can find all saved recipes under More → Favorites & Saved.
How do I add items to my Shopping List? ▾
Go to the Shopping List page from the home screen or bottom nav. Tap the + Add Item button, enter the item name (required) and optional price, then confirm. Items are saved automatically. You can check them off or delete them as needed.
How do I track my nutrition progress? ▾
Your daily nutrition is tracked automatically when you scan meals. View your progress on the Home page where you'll see a health score ring, calorie and macronutrient bars, and a meal history timeline. You can also check your water intake and weight trends in the same section.
How do I update my profile? ▾
Tap More in the bottom navigation, then select Settings. From there you can update your name, dietary preferences, allergies, weight, height, and nutrition goals. Changes are saved to your account automatically.

Troubleshooting

Common issues and how to resolve them quickly.

Camera permission issues ▾
If the camera doesn't open when you tap Scan, your browser or device may be blocking camera access. On desktop: click the camera icon in the address bar and select "Allow". On mobile: go to Settings → Apps → Browser → Permissions and enable Camera. After granting permission, reload the page and try again.
Login problems ▾
Make sure you're using the correct email and password. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in screen to reset it. For persistent issues, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser. If the problem continues, contact support at mybitewise@gmail.com.
Data not loading ▾
If your nutrition data, recipes, or shopping list aren't appearing, try refreshing the page. Check that you're signed in to the correct account. Data syncs from our servers, so a stable internet connection is required. If the issue persists, try logging out and back in.
Internet connection problems ▾
BiteWise requires an active internet connection for most features, including AI scanning, recipe discovery, and data sync. If you're experiencing connectivity issues, check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection. Some features like viewing previously loaded recipes may work in limited offline mode.

Account & Privacy

Manage your account and understand how your data is protected.

👤

Managing Your Account

Your account stores your profile info, preferences, and nutrition history. You can update your name, email, dietary preferences, and goals at any time from the Settings page.

🔒

Data Protection

Your health and nutrition data is private and secure. BiteWise uses encryption to protect your information both in transit and at rest. We never share your personal data with third parties without your consent.

🚪

Logging Out

To log out of your account, go to More → Settings and tap the Sign Out button. You'll be returned to the sign-in screen. Your data remains saved and will be available when you sign back in.

⚙️

Updating Preferences

Customize your BiteWise experience by updating dietary preferences, allergies, nutrition goals, and theme (dark/light mode) from the Settings and Health Profile sections.

🔐 Privacy First. BiteWise is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect only the data needed to provide personalized nutrition insights. You can request data deletion at any time by contacting our support team.
