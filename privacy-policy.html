This Privacy Policy explains what information BiteWise collects when you use the BiteWise nutrition and wellness application (the "Service"), how that information is used and protected, and the choices you have. We are committed to handling your data transparently and only collecting what we need to make the Service work. Our Terms of Use are incorporated by reference.

1 Information We Collect

Information you provide when creating an account. When you sign up for BiteWise, we collect the email address and password you use to create your account (your password is processed and stored by our authentication and account services — we never see or store your plaintext password), your display name, and the account type you select (standard user or healthcare provider).

Email verification data. When you verify your email, we store a temporary verification token and a record of whether your email address has been verified.

Health profile and onboarding information. When you complete your profile, we collect information you choose to provide, including your nutrition goal, health conditions, dietary preferences, allergies, food dislikes or restrictions, and — if you enter them — your age, weight, and height. This information is used to personalize meal plans and recommendations.

Content you provide through the Service.

  • Meal photos: when you use the AI food scanner, the photo you submit is processed to identify foods and estimate nutrition. Photos are used for analysis only and are not stored on our servers or saved to your account.
  • Nutrition tracking: meal history, estimated nutrition values (calories, macros, fiber, sugar, sodium, health scores), water intake logs, weight logs, and meal plans you generate.
  • Chat messages: messages you send to AI chat features, which are used to generate responses and provide your chat history.
  • Other activity: saved favorites, shopping lists, and challenges you join.

Healthcare provider accounts. If you use the Service as a healthcare provider, we may collect the professional information you provide and any patient notes or analysis you create, which you should only create in accordance with your own legal and ethical obligations.

Device and technical information. We do not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Like most web services, our infrastructure provider's servers may log basic technical information (such as IP address, request time, and browser type) to operate and secure the Service.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Creating and authenticating your account, and sending verification emails;
  • Providing the features you use, including food recognition, nutrition estimates, meal plans, tracking, chat, shopping lists, and challenges;
  • Personalizing recommendations based on your goal, preferences, and history;
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the Service, including diagnosing errors and preventing abuse;
  • Communicating with you about your account, service changes, and support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3 Local Storage and Cookies

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to store lightweight preferences on your device. Specifically, we store:

  • Your user identifier and session token (to keep you signed in and route you to the correct page);
  • A cached copy of your profile and onboarding preferences (to avoid re-downloading them on every visit);
  • Temporary UI state, such as a short-term navigation guard used to prevent redirect loops and the email address you used when a verification email was requested.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to third parties. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings; doing so will sign you out of the Service.

4 Third-Party Services We Rely On

To operate the Service, we rely on certain external service providers to support some of its features. These services process limited data on our behalf. We describe them by function below rather than by name, because the specific providers may change over time:

  • Authentication and account services — used for secure account creation, sign-in, and account management.
  • Storage services — used to store and manage profile, onboarding, and application-related data.
  • AI services — used to generate meal plans, recommendations, chat responses, and food or nutrition analysis based on information you provide through the Service.
  • Nutrition reference services — used to provide public food and nutrition reference information that helps estimate or display nutrition facts.
  • Email delivery services — used to send account verification emails, OTP/verification codes, and service-related emails.

These service providers have their own privacy policies and may process data in countries other than your own. We only share the minimum information needed to provide the features you use.

5 Data Retention

We keep your account data (profile, meal history, tracking logs, chat history, and preferences) for as long as your account is active, so that your history remains available to you. Verification tokens are kept only until the email is verified. Temporary technical logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operations.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting mybitewise@gmail.com, and we will delete your data from our systems and databases, subject to legal retention obligations.

6 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Transport encryption (HTTPS) for all communication with the Service;
  • Authentication handled by our authentication and account services, with passwords never stored in plaintext;
  • Access controls and secure credential management on our servers;
  • Email verification on account creation to reduce fraudulent signups.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can help by keeping your password confidential and reporting suspected unauthorized access.

7 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or comparable laws), you may have rights including:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correction: update or correct inaccurate information (you can also edit much of your profile directly in the app);
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and data;
  • Restriction and objection: object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • Portability: request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mybitewise@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at mybitewise@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete the information as required by law.
